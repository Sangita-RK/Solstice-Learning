# D-04: Understanding Billing Cycles

Your billing cycle starts on the day you first subscribed and renews monthly on that same date. Invoices are emailed to your billing contact 3 days before each renewal. You can update your billing contact or payment method anytime from Settings > Billing. If a renewal charge fails, we'll retry twice over the following week before pausing your account. Refunds for annual plans canceled within the first 30 days are available on request.
